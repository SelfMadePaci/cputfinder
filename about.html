About CPUT Finder

CPUT Finder helps students and staff find their way around the CPUT District 6 campus.

The website includes a campus map, saved places feature, a timetable, cafeteria information and a profile section.

This project was developed by Group 5 for Project 2 (PRT262S) at Cape Peninsula University of Technology

How to Use the App

  • Map: View the campus map and tap on markers to get information about each building.
  • Saved Places: Save places that you frequently visit or want to access quickly.
  • My Timetable: Add and edit your weekly class schedule.
  • Cafeteria: View the cafeteria menu and available food options.
  • Profile: View and update your personal information.
